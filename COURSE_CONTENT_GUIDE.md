## 2. 4-Step Problem Anatomy

Every problem presented to learners follows the 4-step pedagogical structure:
1. **Problem Statement:** Clear, approachable scenario with explicit inputs and outputs.
2. **Logic Behind the Solution:** Explains the conceptual mechanism before writing syntax.
3. **Step-by-Step Algorithm:** Pseudocode breaking down actions sequentially.
4. **Scaffolded Editor:** Guided practice across Mode A (Trace), Mode B (Fill-in), or Mode C (Independent).
